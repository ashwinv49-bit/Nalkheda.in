Nalkheda has no railway station of its own. The last stretch is always by road.

सबसे नज़दीकी स्टेशन ही सबसे अच्छा नहीं है
The Closest Station Is Not the Best Station

This is the part most pilgrims get wrong, and it costs them an evening. On a map, Berchha sits about 60 km from Nalkheda and Ujjain Junction sits at 100 km, so Berchha looks like the obvious choice. In practice it rarely is.

Berchha and Shajapur are small stations. Few express trains stop there, the ones that do often pass at inconvenient hours, and there is no dependable taxi rank waiting outside. Arriving at a quiet Malwa station at 11 PM with no onward transport is a genuinely difficult situation, particularly with family or luggage.

उज्जैन जंक्शन 40 किलोमीटर ज़्यादा दूर है, लेकिन वहाँ ट्रेनें ज़्यादा हैं, टैक्सी और बस दोनों आसानी से मिलती हैं, और महाकालेश्वर के दर्शन भी उसी यात्रा में हो जाते हैं। इसीलिए ज़्यादातर श्रद्धालु उज्जैन ही उतरते हैं।

छोटा नियम — A simple rule

Book to Ujjain Junction unless a specific train drops you at Berchha or Shajapur in daylight and you have already arranged a taxi for the onward leg. The extra 40 km is bought back many times over in reliability.

उज्जैन स्टेशन से नलखेड़ा कैसे पहुँचें
From Ujjain Junction to Nalkheda — Bus and Taxi

Ujjain Junction to Nalkheda is 100 km on NH52, through Agar Malwa. The road is in reasonable condition and the drive takes about two hours in a car.

🚌
बस से — सबसे किफ़ायती
By bus — the cheapest option

MPSRTC buses leave from Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Bus Stand, a short auto ride from the railway station. Services run roughly every 2–3 hours.

💰 किराया: ₹80 – ₹120 ⏱️ समय: 2.5 – 3 hrs
🚕
टैक्सी से — सबसे आरामदायक
By taxi — fastest and most comfortable

Worth it for a family or if you are arriving late. Agree the fare before starting, and confirm whether it covers the return or a wait at the temple.

💰 किराया: ₹3,000 – ₹5,000 one way ⏱️ समय: ~2 hrs via NH52
💬 टैक्सी की जानकारी लें

अगर आप महाकालेश्वर के दर्शन भी करना चाहते हैं तो उज्जैन में एक रात रुककर अगले दिन नलखेड़ा जाना सबसे सुविधाजनक रहता है। दोनों दर्शन एक ही यात्रा में हो जाते हैं।

ट्रेन से आ रहे हैं तो समय का ध्यान रखें
Timing Your Arrival Around the Aarti

Darshan at Nalkheda runs from 6:00 AM to 9:30 PM, with the morning aarti at 6:00 AM and the evening aarti at 7:30 PM. Because the road leg is two hours, your train time decides which of those you can realistically attend.

उज्जैन पहुँचने का समय / Arrival at Ujjainक्या संभव है / What works
सुबह जल्दी (5–7 AM) Reach Nalkheda by mid-morning for a comfortable darshan, and return to Ujjain the same evening. No overnight stay needed.
दोपहर (12–3 PM) You will arrive in time for the 7:30 PM evening aarti. Plan to stay the night rather than driving back in the dark.
शाम या रात Stay in Ujjain overnight and drive out early next morning. Attempting the road leg late at night is not worth it.
6 AM आरती करनी है Stay near the temple the previous night. From Ujjain you would need to leave around 4:00 AM — see hotels near the temple.
हवन करवाना है? — Planning a hawan?

The hawan booking counter at the temple works in person, 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM, and a hawan takes several hours. If that is your reason for travelling, arrive the day before rather than trying to fit it around a train. Booking is handled by the temple trust at mabaglamukhi.com.

रेल के अलावा
If Rail Does Not Suit You

The nearest airport is Indore (IDR), 156 km away, with a road journey of about 3 to 3.5 hours. Direct buses also run to Nalkheda from Indore's Sarwate Bus Terminal (₹120–180, ~3.5 hours) and from Ujjain, Dewas and Agar Malwa.

Full route details, fares and drive times from every major city are on the how to reach Nalkheda page.

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the nearest railway station to Nalkheda?
+

The nearest major railway station to Nalkheda is Ujjain Junction (UJN), about 100 km away and roughly 2 hours by road. Two smaller stations are physically closer — Berchha (about 60 km) and Shajapur (about 65 km) — but both have limited train connectivity and few onward taxis, so most pilgrims still arrive at Ujjain Junction.

Is there a railway station in Nalkheda itself?
+

No. Nalkheda town has no railway station of its own. The final stretch of the journey is by road from whichever station you arrive at. From Ujjain Junction the route is NH52 via Agar Malwa.

How do I get from Ujjain Junction to Nalkheda Baglamukhi temple?
+

By bus from Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Bus Stand in Ujjain: services run every 2-3 hours, cost ₹80-120 and take 2.5-3 hours. By taxi: ₹3,000-5,000 one way, about 2 hours via NH52 through Agar Malwa. The bus stand is a short auto ride from the railway station.

Should I get down at Berchha or Shajapur instead of Ujjain?
+

Usually no. Berchha (60 km) and Shajapur (65 km) are closer to Nalkheda but are small stations where few express trains stop and pre-booked taxis are scarce, especially at night. Ujjain Junction has far more trains, reliable onward transport, and lets you combine the trip with Mahakaleshwar darshan.

Which trains go to Ujjain Junction?
+

Ujjain Junction is a major station on the Western Railway network with direct trains from Indore, Bhopal, Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Jaipur and Nagpur. Nagda Junction, on the Delhi-Mumbai main line, is an alternative arrival point if your train does not stop at Ujjain; from Nagda it is roughly a 2.5 hour taxi ride to Nalkheda. Check live timings on the IRCTC website or NTES before booking.

Can I reach Nalkheda in time for the 6 AM aarti if I arrive by train?
+

Only if you stay overnight. Even an early train into Ujjain leaves a 2 hour road journey, so a 6:00 AM aarti means starting from Ujjain around 4:00 AM. Most pilgrims arrive the previous evening and stay near the temple, or take an Ujjain hotel and attend a later darshan instead.
