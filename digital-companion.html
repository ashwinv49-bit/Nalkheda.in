FAQ

Common Questions

Is this a travel agency? +
No. Nalkheda.in is an independent digital information and planning service. We are not a registered travel agency or tour operator. We provide personalized guidance, local knowledge, and recommendations. All travel decisions, bookings, and payments remain entirely in your hands.
Do you book hotels on my behalf? +
No. We provide trusted hotel and guesthouse recommendations tailored to your budget and group size, with real distance from the temple and honest descriptions. All reservations are made directly by you with the property. We share contacts and help you know what questions to ask.
Can you arrange VIP Darshan? +
No. VIP Darshan arrangements at Maa Baglamukhi Temple, Nalkheda, are managed exclusively by the temple administration through mabaglamukhi.com. We can advise you on how to apply for it, but we have no role in that booking process.
Can I ask questions during my actual trip? +
Yes — that is one of the core features of the Digital Companion. WhatsApp support is available to you for up to 2 days from your travel date. Ask about queue lengths, route alternatives, food near the temple, parking, or anything else you encounter on the ground.
How long is the WhatsApp support valid? +
Support is valid for 2 days starting from your stated travel date. We recommend sharing your travel date accurately at booking so we can be available on the right days. If your trip extends longer, extended support can be arranged separately.
Do you help NRIs or travellers from outside India? +
Yes. A significant number of our travellers are NRIs, PIOs, and international visitors unfamiliar with local transport, currency, or temple customs. We are experienced in planning journeys for those navigating from outside India and understand the additional context needed.
Can families with senior citizens use this service? +
Absolutely. Senior citizen guidance is a dedicated part of what we provide — including accessible entry routes, rest stop locations, queue-avoidance strategies, and which darshan and hawan options are suitable for those with limited mobility. Families are our most common use case.
