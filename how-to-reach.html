Most Popular Route

Ujjain to Nalkheda (100 km)

Most pilgrims combine Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga in Ujjain with Maa Baglamukhi Nalkheda on the same trip. Ujjain is the nearest major city and transport hub.

🚌 By Bus Most Economical

FromDevi Ahilyabai Holkar Bus Stand, Ujjain
FrequencyEvery 2–3 hours
Journey Time2.5 – 3 hours
Cost₹80 – ₹120
RouteVia Agar Malwa (SH27)

MPSRTC state buses are the cheapest option. First bus departs around 5:30–6:00 AM. Also private mini-buses available from near the bus stand.

🚕 By Taxi / Cab Most Comfortable

Private Taxi₹1,500 – ₹2,000 (one way)
Round Trip₹2,500 – ₹3,500
Shared Cab₹150–₹200/person

Book viaWhatsApp / local agents

Ola/Uber may not have direct Nalkheda routes. Book through local taxi operators or contact us on WhatsApp for cab help.

Ujjain to Nalkheda Road Route

Ujjain → Badnagar → Agar Malwa → Nalkheda
Take NH52 from Ujjain towards Ratlam, then turn onto SH27 at Badnagar towards Agar. From Agar, it's 35 km to Nalkheda on a well-paved state highway. Total approx 100 km, 2 hours in normal traffic.

From Indore

Indore to Nalkheda (150 km)

🚌 By Bus

FromSarwate Bus Terminal, Indore
FrequencyMultiple daily
Journey Time3.5 – 4 hours
Cost₹120 – ₹180

Buses go via Dewas and Agar Malwa. Change at Agar if needed for local bus to Nalkheda. Direct Indore–Nalkheda buses are also available but less frequent.

🚕 By Taxi

Private Taxi₹2,000 – ₹2,500 (one way)
Round Trip₹3,500 – ₹4,500
Journey Time~3 hours

Many pilgrims do a Indore–Ujjain–Nalkheda 2-day circuit. Hire a round-trip cab from Indore for convenience.

Indore to Nalkheda Road Route

Indore → Dewas → Shajapur → Agar Malwa → Nalkheda
Take NH52 from Indore towards Dewas (35 km). Continue on NH52 past Shajapur towards Agar Malwa (125 km from Indore). From Agar take SH27 to Nalkheda (150 km total). Highway is well-maintained. Fuel stations available at Dewas and Agar.

Rail Connection

Nearest Railway Stations

Nalkheda does not have its own railway station. The nearest stations are listed below. From any of these stations, take a bus or pre-booked taxi to Nalkheda.

🚆 Ujjain Junction (UJN)

Distance to Nalkheda100 km
Major lineDelhi–Mumbai (via Kota)
Trains from DelhiAvantika Exp, Malwa Exp, JP SF Exp
Trains from MumbaiAvantika Exp, Pushpak Exp

From Ujjain station, take a taxi (~₹1,800) or MPSRTC bus to Nalkheda. Most pilgrims use Ujjain as their base and do day-trips to Nalkheda.

🚆 Nagda Junction (NAD)

Distance to Nalkheda~120 km
Major lineNagda–Ratlam–Bhopal
OptionTake bus to Agar, then onward to Nalkheda

Less convenient than Ujjain for most travellers. Useful if arriving from Ratlam or Bhopal direction.

By Air

Nearest Airports

✈️ Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport — Indore (IDR)

Distance to Nalkheda150 km (~3 hrs)
Flights fromDelhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Kolkata
OnwardPre-book taxi from airport: ₹2,500–₹3,000

Recommended gateway. Indore has more frequent flights from major cities. Hire a cab from Indore airport directly to Nalkheda or combine with Ujjain visit.

✈️ Ujjain Airport (Under Development)

StatusLimited / seasonal flights
AlternativeFly to Indore, drive to Ujjain (55 km)

Ujjain's airport has limited connectivity. Indore remains the preferred air gateway for pilgrims visiting Nalkheda.
