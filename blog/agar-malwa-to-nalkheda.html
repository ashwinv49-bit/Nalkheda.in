Agar Malwa to Nalkheda — 35 km Route Guide

Updated August 2026 · Verified distances, fares and departure times

Agar Malwa to Nalkheda is just 35 km — about 40 minutes by road. It is the closest town of any size to Maa Baglamukhi Mandir, which makes it the practical fallback whenever Nalkheda itself is full, and the obvious base during Navratri.

Quick Answer

  • Distance: ~35 km
  • Driving time: ~40 minutes (1–1.5 hrs during Navratri)
  • Route: Agar Malwa → Nalkheda road, well signposted
  • Taxi: ₹800–1,200 one way
  • Bus: frequent, ₹30–50, about 45 minutes

The Shortest Approach to the Temple

Agar Malwa is the district headquarters and the last town of any size before Nalkheda. The road is straightforward and well signposted — most drivers in Agar know the temple without needing directions, and the dome and orange flags are visible from the main road as you arrive.

Why Agar Malwa Matters More Than Its Size Suggests

Two reasons, and both become obvious at exactly the wrong moment if you have not planned for them.

1. It is where you stay when Nalkheda is full

Nalkheda has limited rooms and no dharamshala. During Navratri and on Purnima weekends it fills first. Agar Malwa has more hotels at ₹1,200–1,500 a night, generally better rooms, and it is only 40 minutes away — close enough to still make the 6 AM Mangala Aarti with a 5 AM start.

2. It is the junction for onward travel

Buses from Ujjain, Indore, Dewas and Bhopal pass through or terminate at Agar. If you are travelling by bus, you will almost certainly change here. It also has the nearest district hospital, which matters if you are travelling with elderly pilgrims.

During Navratri, double it

For 11–20 October 2026, allow 1–1.5 hours for the same 35 km. Traffic on the approach road slows badly and parking moves further out each day of the festival.

By Bus

Buses run frequently between Agar Malwa and Nalkheda — roughly every 30 to 60 minutes through the day, ₹30–50, about 45 minutes. This is the cheapest reliable way to reach the temple and is used by most local pilgrims. Shared autos also run the route.

By Taxi

₹800–1,200 one way. For a family or a group this is worth it over the bus, particularly for an early start when buses are thin. Agree the fare in writing, and ask whether the driver will wait — for a 35 km hop, a wait-and-return arrangement is usually better value than two separate trips.

A Sensible Two-Day Plan Based in Agar

Day 1

Arrive Agar Malwa, check in. Drive to Nalkheda in the afternoon, attend Sandhya Aarti at 7:30 PM, return to Agar for the night.

Day 2

Leave at 5:00 AM for Mangala Aarti at 6:00 AM. Hawan afterwards if booked. Breakfast in Nalkheda, then onward.

You see the shrine both full and empty, twelve hours apart, and sleep in a better room than Nalkheda can offer. For most families this beats staying in Nalkheda itself.

Hawan still needs advance booking

Being 35 km away does not help with hawan. Book at mabaglamukhi.com or the temple counter — a week ahead normally, three months for Navratri. We do not book hawan.

Also Worth Knowing

  • ATMs — withdraw in Agar. Nalkheda's are limited and often out of service during festivals.
  • Yellow clothing — buy it in Agar if you have not brought it. Nalkheda's market sells out during Navratri.
  • Medical — Agar has the nearest district hospital. Nalkheda has only a primary health centre.
  • Baijnath Mahadev temple in Agar is worth an hour if you have the time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Agar Malwa from Nalkheda?

Agar Malwa to Nalkheda is approximately 35 km, about 40 minutes by road. During Navratri allow 1 to 1.5 hours.

Is there a bus from Agar Malwa to Nalkheda?

Yes, buses run frequently — roughly every 30 to 60 minutes through the day. The fare is ₹30–50 and the journey takes about 45 minutes. Shared autos also run the route.

Should I stay in Agar Malwa or Nalkheda?

Agar Malwa has better hotels at ₹1,200–1,500 per night and is only 40 minutes away, so you can still make the 6 AM Mangala Aarti with a 5 AM start. Nalkheda's guesthouses are ₹1,000–1,500 but limited, and there is no dharamshala. During Navratri, Agar is often the only option.

What is the taxi fare from Agar Malwa to Nalkheda?

Around ₹800–1,200 one way. For a 35 km trip, a wait-and-return arrangement is usually better value than booking two separate one-way trips.

What time should I leave Agar Malwa for the morning aarti?

Leave by 5:00 AM to reach Nalkheda for the 6:00 AM Mangala Aarti — the quietest and best hour of the temple day.
