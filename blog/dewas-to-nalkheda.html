Dewas to Nalkheda — 115 km Route Guide

Updated August 2026 · Verified distances, fares and departure times

Dewas to Nalkheda is about 115 km and takes roughly 2 hours by road via Agar Malwa. It is one of the easier approaches to Maa Baglamukhi Mandir — a single clear route, decent road surface, and short enough to do as a comfortable day trip.

Quick Answer

  • Distance: ~115 km
  • Driving time: ~2 hours (2.5–3 hrs during Navratri)
  • Route: Dewas → SH-52 → Agar Malwa → Nalkheda
  • Taxi: ₹3,500–4,500 one way
  • Bus: change at Agar Malwa, ₹90–140 total

The Route from Dewas

Head out of Dewas on SH-52 towards Agar Malwa. Agar is the natural halfway marker at around 80 km, and from there Nalkheda is a further 35 km on a well-signposted road. You do not need to navigate through Ujjain or Indore, which is what makes this route quicker than the distance suggests.

Route Milestones

  • Dewas → Sonkatch: ~30 km, open highway
  • Sonkatch → Agar Malwa: ~50 km, steady rural road
  • Agar Malwa → Nalkheda: ~35 km, about 40 minutes
  • Nalkheda bus stand → temple: 1.5 km, auto ₹20–40

Which Departure Time Actually Works

This is the part maps will not tell you. Nalkheda's temple day is built around two aartis, and the one you aim for decides when you leave Dewas.

To attendLeave Dewas byWhy
Mangala Aarti, 6:00 AM3:45 AMThe quietest darshan of the day. Worth the early start.
Mid-morning darshan7:00 AMRelaxed, arrive around 9 AM before the midday heat
Sandhya Aarti, 7:30 PM4:45 PMThe main aarti — arrive by 7:15 for a good position

Add an hour during Navratri

For 11–20 October 2026, add 45–60 minutes to every figure above. The approach road becomes a market and parking moves progressively further from the gate as the festival goes on.

By Bus from Dewas

There is no frequent direct service. The reliable method is to take a bus to Agar Malwa (₹60–100, around 1.5 hours), then a local bus or shared auto onward to Nalkheda (₹30–50, about 45 minutes). Total is roughly ₹90–140 and 2.5–3 hours including the change.

Buses are cheap and reliable but slow, and they stop often. Good for a solo pilgrim; hard work with elderly relatives or small children.

By Taxi from Dewas

Expect ₹3,500–4,500 one way for a standard sedan, and ask for a return fare — operators price a round trip considerably better than two one-ways, and you will want a ride back.

Settle these before you set off

  • The total fare in writing — WhatsApp is fine and is evidence
  • What is included — toll, parking, driver allowance
  • How long the driver will wait at the temple, and the charge beyond it
  • Driver name and vehicle number, shared with someone at home

Is a Day Trip Realistic?

Yes, and comfortably so. At 115 km each way you can leave Dewas at 4 AM, attend Mangala Aarti, complete darshan, and be home by early afternoon.

The exception is a hawan. Hawan slots run from 7 AM and the ritual itself takes time — if you have booked one, leave earlier or stay the night in Nalkheda. Trying to fit a hawan into a tight day trip is where most plans come apart.

Book the hawan before you fix the date

Hawan is booked only at mabaglamukhi.com or the temple counter (7 AM–11 PM). A week ahead is enough on an ordinary day; three months for Navratri. Nalkheda.in does not book hawan.

Where to Stay if You Do Stay

There is no dharamshala at Nalkheda — a common and costly assumption. Guesthouses near the temple run ₹1,000–1,500 a night. Agar Malwa (35 km) has better rooms at ₹1,200–1,500. For real comfort, Ujjain at 100 km. See our Nalkheda stay guide.

Making More of the Trip

Dewas sits conveniently for the wider circuit. Ujjain is ~35 km from Dewas, so many pilgrims pair Mahakaleshwar with Nalkheda across two days. See the Ujjain to Nalkheda route or the Nalkheda to Omkareshwar leg if you are building the full MP circuit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Dewas from Nalkheda?

Dewas to Nalkheda is approximately 115 km by road, about 2 hours via SH-52 through Agar Malwa. During Navratri allow 2.5 to 3 hours.

What is the taxi fare from Dewas to Nalkheda?

A standard sedan costs roughly ₹3,500–4,500 one way. Ask for a return fare, which operators price better than two separate one-way trips. Agree the total in writing before departure.

Can I visit Nalkheda from Dewas in one day?

Yes. Leave Dewas by 3:45 AM for the 6:00 AM Mangala Aarti, complete darshan, and return by early afternoon. If you have booked a hawan, allow extra time or stay overnight.

Is there a direct bus from Dewas to Nalkheda?

There is no frequent direct service. Take a bus to Agar Malwa (₹60–100, about 1.5 hours), then a local bus or shared auto to Nalkheda (₹30–50, about 45 minutes).

What time should I leave Dewas for the evening aarti?

Leave by 4:45 PM to reach for Sandhya Aarti at 7:30 PM, arriving around 7:15 for a good position. Add an hour during Navratri.
