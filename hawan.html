How to Book — Two Ways, Both the Temple's

There are exactly two legitimate ways to arrange a hawan at Nalkheda, and both run through the temple trust itself.

🖥️
ऑनलाइन — आधिकारिक वेबसाइट
Online, at the trust's own site

Book at mabaglamukhi.com, run by the Shaskiya Maa Baglamukhi Mandir Prabandh Samiti. This is the only official online channel.

🔱 Open mabaglamukhi.com
🛕
काउंटर पर — व्यक्तिगत रूप से
In person, at the temple counter

The hawan booking counter is open 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM, every day of the year. Go the evening before if you want a slot the next morning — doing it on the day is how people lose one.

🕖 काउंटर: 7:00 AM – 11:00 PM 📅 365 days
We cannot book a hawan for you

Nalkheda.in is an independent guide, not the temple. We do not take bookings and we do not accept payment for any ritual. If anyone — a website, a hotel desk, a WhatsApp forward — offers to secure a hawan for a fee, that is not the temple. Book at mabaglamukhi.com or at the counter, in your own name.

Planning the Day Around It

A hawan takes three to four hours. Pilgrims routinely book a cab for midday, or a train that evening, and then spend the whole ritual watching the clock. Leave the day open.

  • Arrive the night before. Confirm your slot at the counter that evening, not on the morning itself.
  • Take the 6:00 AM aarti first if you are staying near the temple — it is the quietest hour of the day.
  • Do not book travel for that afternoon. If you must leave the same day, make it an evening departure from Ujjain, not a midday one.
  • Two nights if you can. One for the counter and the aarti, one for the hawan itself without a deadline.
नवरात्रि 2026 — 11 से 20 अक्टूबर

Baglamukhi is a Navratri deity and this is the busiest stretch of the year. The 75 kunds and the rooms near the temple both go early — arrange both by around July if you intend to travel then. Baglamukhi Jayanti (Vaishakh Shukla Ashtami) is the temple's other peak.

रुकने की व्यवस्था
Where to Stay

A hawan effectively requires an overnight stay. There is no dharamshala at Nalkheda, though the temple committee runs a guest house bookable through mabaglamukhi.com — worth trying first. Private guesthouses near the temple run about ₹1,000–1,500 a night.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a hawan at Nalkheda Baglamukhi temple?
+

Booking is handled only by the temple trust. Book online at mabaglamukhi.com, the Shaskiya Maa Baglamukhi Mandir Prabandh Samiti's own website, or in person at the hawan booking counter at the temple, which is open 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Nalkheda.in cannot book a hawan for you and does not accept payment for one.

What are the hawan booking counter timings?
+

The hawan booking counter at the temple is open from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM, every day of the year. Darshan itself runs 6:00 AM to 9:30 PM. During Navratri the temple stays open later.

What do I need to bring to book a hawan?
+

Valid photo ID — an Aadhaar card is the usual one — for each person named. The booking is recorded against a name and gotra, so know your gotra before you reach the counter. Samagri requirements vary by the type of hawan; ask at the counter when you book.

How much does a hawan cost at Nalkheda?
+

The temple charges roughly ₹1,100 to ₹5,100 or more as an advance, depending on the type of hawan performed. The fee is set by the temple trust and paid to the trust, not to any agent. Treat anyone quoting a different fixed price, or asking you to pay them instead, with caution.

How long does a hawan take?
+

Allow three to four hours. This is the single most common planning mistake pilgrims make at Nalkheda — booking travel for immediately afterwards. A hawan cannot be hurried, and the day it falls on should be left otherwise clear.

How many hawan kunds are there at Nalkheda?
+

There are 75 hawan kunds at Maa Baglamukhi Mandir, Nalkheda. They are in demand through Navratri and on Baglamukhi Jayanti, so book well ahead for those dates.

Can I have a hawan performed during Navratri?
+

Yes, and it is the busiest time of the year. Sharad Navratri 2026 runs from 11 to 20 October. Kunds and nearby rooms both book out well in advance for those dates — arrange both by around July if you intend to travel then.
