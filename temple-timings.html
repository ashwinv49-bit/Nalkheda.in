Daily Schedule at a Glance

Maa Baglamukhi Temple Timings

🌅

Darshan (General)

6:00 AM – 9:30 PM

Temple open daily for general darshan. Gates open at sunrise. Last entry 9:15 PM.

🔔

Morning Aarti

6:00 AM

Mangla Aarti with Panchamrit Abhishekam. Arrive by 5:40 AM for a close darshan spot.

🪔

Evening Aarti

7:30 PM

Sandhya Aarti — the most spiritually charged aarti of the day. Arrive by 7:00 PM.

🌞

Noon Bhog Aarti

12:00 PM

Madhyanha Aarti and Rajbhog Bhog offering. Brief closure for 30 min after for ritual cleaning.

🔥

Hawan (Yagna)

7:00 AM – 11:00 PM

75 hawan kunds available. Each slot is 2–3 hours. Advance booking strongly recommended.

🌙

Navratri Extended

Till 11 PM–12 AM

During Navratri (Chaitra & Sharad), temple stays open till midnight. Aarti at 10:30 PM.

Best Time for Darshan

  • Early morning (6:00 – 8:00 AM) — Least crowded, fresh and spiritually peaceful, aarti blessings
  • Evening (7:00 – 8:30 PM) — Sandhya Aarti ambiance, beautiful temple lighting
  • Weekday mornings — Avoid weekends and festival days if you want a short queue
Festival & Special Days

Extended Festival Timings

During major festivals the temple stays open significantly longer and sees massive pilgrim attendance. Plan your visit and accommodation accordingly.

Navratri (Chaitra)

Chaitra Navratri 2026: March 30 – April 7. The most spiritually significant Navratri. Special 9-day decoration, all 75 hawan kunds lit daily, free Bhandara (food) for pilgrims.

Open: 5:00 AM – 12:00 AM (Midnight)

Navratri (Sharad / Ashwin)

Sharad Navratri 2026: Oct 2 – Oct 10. Biggest festival with 50,000–100,000 pilgrims expected. Massive celebration, evening Garba, late-night aarti.

Open: 4:00 AM – 12:00 AM (Midnight)

Baglamukhi Jayanti 2026

Estimated: May 27, 2026 (Vaishakha Shukla Ashtami). Birthday celebration of Maa Baglamukhi. Special Mahayagya, Mahabhishekam, and overnight puja.

Open: 24 Hours (all day & night)

Purnima & Ekadashi

Every Purnima (full moon) and Ekadashi (11th lunar day) is auspicious. Special abhishekam and extended darshan. Significant extra crowd, especially on Vaishakha Purnima.

Open: Extended till 10:30 PM

Important: Festival Visit Planning

  • Book accommodation 2–3 months in advance for Navratri. All hotels fill up fast.
  • During Navratri darshan queues can be 2–5 hours long. Arrive before 5:00 AM or after 8:00 PM.
  • Hawan kund slots for Navratri open months in advance at mabaglamukhi.com.
  • Traffic and parking: Temple road becomes pedestrian-only during major festivals.
Hawan Yagna

Hawan (Yagna) Timings & Booking

Performing a hawan at Maa Baglamukhi's sacred kunds is the most potent form of worship here. The temple has 75 hawan kunds — one of the largest hawan complexes in India.

75

Hawan Kunds

Sacred fire altars available for pilgrims. Each kund is dedicated to a specific puja intent.

7 AM – 11 PM

Daily Hawan Hours

Slots available from early morning to late evening. Each slot is 2–3 hours long.

Book Online

Advance Booking

Book your kund slot at mabaglamukhi.com. Walk-in available but not guaranteed during festivals.

Hawan Booking Process

  • Visit mabaglamukhi.com for online booking (official Samiti site)
  • Or visit the temple office directly on arrival (Temple Road, Nalkheda)
  • Carry valid ID (Aadhar Card) for registration
  • Dakshina (offering) is separate from slot booking — pandit will guide you
  • Book minimum 1 week ahead; minimum 3 months ahead for Navratri slots
  • Warning: Multiple fake websites claim to offer hawan booking. Only use mabaglamukhi.com (official) or visit in person.
Pilgrims' Guide

Tips for the Perfect Darshan

Arrive Early

For morning aarti darshan, reach the temple by 5:40 AM. The queue forms quickly. If coming from Ujjain (100 km), plan to leave by 3:30 AM for aarti.

Dress in Yellow

Maa Baglamukhi is Pitambara Devi — yellow is her sacred colour. Wear yellow clothes or at minimum carry a yellow dupatta/stole for the visit.

Mobile Phones

Photography may be restricted near the Garbhagriha (inner sanctum). Keep phone on silent mode. Video recording inside the main temple hall is generally not permitted.

What to Carry

Yellow flowers (marigold), haldi (turmeric), coconut, and yellow fruit. Avoid leather items inside the temple. Keep footwear at the designated shoe stand (free, near gate).
