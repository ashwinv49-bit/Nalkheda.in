Accommodation Overview

Where to Stay Near Nalkheda

Nalkheda is a small town with limited but adequate accommodation for pilgrims. Most options are basic and clean — perfectly suited for a 1-2 night pilgrimage stay. For better hotel options and city facilities, many pilgrims use Ujjain (100 km) as their base and do day-trips to Nalkheda.

Key Booking Tips

  • Navratri (March/October): Book at least 2–3 months in advance — all accommodation fills up fast
  • Weekends: Book at least 1–2 weeks ahead; weekdays are much easier
  • Dharamshala: Free but limited space — call ahead to confirm availability
  • Ujjain base: Better hotel options, more comfortable; do Nalkheda as a day trip
Free / Donation-Based

Dharamshalas & Temple Guest House

FREE · DONATION BASIS

Maa Baglamukhi Dharamshala

The temple trust operates a dharamshala on the temple campus for pilgrims. Basic facilities including fan rooms, clean washrooms, and drinking water. No charge — donations welcome. Priority given to those performing hawan or multi-day sadhana.

Facilities: Dormitory & some private rooms, fan, water, toilets, vegetarian langar on festival days

📍 Temple Campus, Nalkheda  |  Contact via mabaglamukhi.com or temple office

FREE · COMMUNITY RUN

Other Community Dharamshalas

Several community and trust-run dharamshalas are located within 0.5–1 km of the temple. These are run by religious trusts for pilgrims visiting Nalkheda. Facilities vary from basic to moderate.

How to find: Ask at the temple entrance or in the main bazaar near the temple. Locals are very helpful in directing pilgrims.

What to Expect in Nalkheda Hotels

  • Most hotels serve pure vegetarian food only — no non-veg available near temple
  • Basic amenities: fan, attached bath, hot water. AC available in mid-range options
  • Generally clean and adequate for a pilgrimage stay of 1–2 nights
  • Limited WiFi — carry mobile data
  • Most hotels have on-site parking for cars
Best Base for Your Pilgrimage

Stay in Ujjain — The Better Base Option

If you want more comfort, better food options, and city facilities, Ujjain (100 km from Nalkheda) is an excellent base. You can also visit Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga, Harsiddhi Mata, and other temples — making it a true pilgrimage circuit.

Ujjain as Base — 2-Day Itinerary Idea

  • Day 1: Arrive Ujjain, check in, Mahakaleshwar Bhasma Aarti (4:30 AM) or evening darshan, explore Ujjain temples (Harsiddhi, Kaal Bhairav)
  • Day 2: Early morning depart for Nalkheda (6 AM), attend Mangla Aarti at 6 AM or just after, do hawan, explore temple, return to Ujjain evening
  • Distance Ujjain–Nalkheda: 100 km, 2 hours by car
