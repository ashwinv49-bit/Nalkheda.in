About the temple

Maa Baglamukhi Mandir at Nalkheda stands on the bank of the Lakhundar Nadi — called Lakshmana Nadi in older texts — in Agar Malwa district, Madhya Pradesh. It is revered as a Jagrut Shakti Peeth and is one of three principal Baglamukhi temples in India.

Pilgrims come chiefly for the hawan. The temple has 75 kunds and the booking counter runs from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM, every day of the year.

Darshan6:00 AM – 9:30 PM, daily
Mangala Aarti6:00 AM
Bhog Aarti12:00 PM
Sandhya Aarti7:30 PM
Shayan Aarti9:00 PM
AddressChapiheda Road, Nalkheda, Agar Malwa, MP 465445

Hawan booking: at the temple counter in person, or online at the official government portal mabaglamukhi.com. We do not book hawan and cannot take payment for it. We can help with travel and stay around your booking.

Ujjain Junction is the best-connected railway station. The nearest airport is Devi Ahilyabai Holkar Airport, Indore. From Nalkheda bus stand the temple is about 1.5 km — a 15-minute walk, or ₹20–40 by auto.
