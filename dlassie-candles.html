Our Story

Crafted with
Passion & Purpose

Dlassie Candles was born from a deep love for the art of slow living. Every candle in our collection is hand-poured with 100% pure soy wax, thoughtfully scented with premium fragrance oils that carry a story in every note.

Based in India, we believe that luxury needn't be imported — it can be cultivated right here, with care, craft, and intention. Each sculpted flower, each layered vessel, each delicate form is shaped by hand and poured with love.

Whether you're gifting a loved one or creating your own sacred space, Dlassie Candles bring warmth, beauty, and a moment of stillness into everyday life.
