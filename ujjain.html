Sacred Sites

Major Temples of Ujjain

Ujjain holds 8 principal temples — plan minimum 2 days for a complete darshan circuit.

⚡
Jyotirlinga #7
Mahakaleshwar Mandir
One of 12 Jyotirlingas — the only south-facing (Dakshinamukhi) Shiva lingam. The Bhasma Aarti at 4AM uses sacred ash in an ancient Tantric ritual. Pre-book at mahakaleshwar.com. A once-in-a-lifetime experience.
⏰ 4AM–11PM  |  🔥 Bhasma Aarti 4AM (pre-book)
🔱
Shakti Peetha
Maa Harsiddhi Shakti Peetha
One of 51 Shaktipeethas where Sati's elbow fell. Famous for two massive oil lamps (deepmalas) lit on Navratri. Linked to King Vikramaditya's legend of devotion.
⏰ 6AM–10PM  |  📍 Near Rudrasagar Lake
🌙
Tantric Temple
Kaal Bhairav Temple
The fierce guardian of Ujjain. Unique tradition of offering liquor (madira) as prasad — the idol is said to actually consume it. A deeply powerful Tantric experience.
⏰ 6AM–10PM  |  📍 North Ujjain
🪐
Navgraha Temple
Mangalnath Temple
Birthplace of planet Mars (Mangal Graha) per Hindu cosmology. Essential for those with Mangal dosha. Special puja on Tuesdays. On the banks of Shipra river.
⏰ 5AM–9PM  |  📍 Shipra River bank
📚
Historical
Sandipani Ashram
The legendary gurukul where Lord Krishna, Balaram and Sudama received education. The stone slab on which Krishna wrote lessons is preserved here.
⏰ 8AM–7PM  |  📍 Ankapata area
🌊
Sacred Ghat
Ram Ghat — Shipra River
Most sacred ghat on Shipra. Evening aarti rivals Varanasi's Ganga Aarti. Kumbh Mela is held here every 12 years — next in 2028.
⏰ Aarti 7PM daily  |  🛁 Sacred bath at dawn

उज्जैन — महाकाल की पावन नगरी

उज्जैन, जिसे प्राचीनकाल में अवंतिका के नाम से जाना जाता था, मध्य प्रदेश की आध्यात्मिक राजधानी है। शिप्रा नदी के तट पर स्थित यह नगर सप्त पुरियों में से एक है और यहाँ भगवान महाकालेश्वर का विश्व प्रसिद्ध ज्योतिर्लिंग स्थित है। यह देश के 12 ज्योतिर्लिंगों में सातवाँ और एकमात्र दक्षिणमुखी शिवलिंग है।

महाकालेश्वर मंदिर में प्रतिदिन सुबह 4 बजे होने वाली भस्म आरती विश्व की अनूठी धार्मिक परंपरा है। इस आरती में जलती चिता की भस्म से शिवलिंग का श्रृंगार किया जाता है। यह आरती देखना एक अलौकिक अनुभव है जो जीवन में एक बार अवश्य करना चाहिए।

उज्जैन से मात्र 100 किमी की दूरी पर नलखेड़ा धाम है जहाँ माँ बगलामुखी का प्रसिद्ध सिद्धपीठ स्थित है, और 130 किमी की दूरी पर ओंकारेश्वर ज्योतिर्लिंग है। उज्जैन को केंद्र मानकर 5 दिनों में इन तीनों पवित्र स्थलों का दर्शन करना संभव है।

Frequently Asked Questions — Ujjain

🕉️ Ujjain is the central hub for the MP Pilgrimage Circuit. Mahakaleshwar Jyotirlinga darshan: 4AM–11PM. Bhasma Aarti at 4AM daily — pre-book at mabaglamukhi.com. Nearest airport: Indore (55 km). Direct trains to Ujjain Junction from Delhi, Mumbai, Bhopal, Kota.

Bhasma Aarti Ujjain subah 4:00 AM ko hoti hai. Online booking: mahakaleshwar.com par 15 din pehle karein. Entry fee: ₹250 per person. Aarti mein shivling par chita bhasma se shringar kiya jaata hai — ek adbhut Tantric anubhav. Limited seats — Navratri mein 1 month pehle book karein.
Indore Airport to Ujjain: ~55 km, ~1 hour. Prepaid cab: ₹800–₹1,200. Trains also available from Indore to Ujjain (~1.5 hrs). Indore has direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Hyderabad. For cab booking WhatsApp: +91 97547 09571.
Hotels near Mahakaleshwar Ujjain: Hotel Shipra Residency (₹1,500–₹3,000), Hotel Malwa Residency (₹800–₹1,500), Dharamshalas (₹300–₹600). Navratri mein 2–3 months pehle book karein. Booking.com aur MMT par best deals milti hain.
Ujjain se Nalkheda: ~100 km, ~2 hrs, ₹3,000–₹5,000 cab. Ujjain se Omkareshwar: ~130 km, ~2.5 hrs, ₹2,000–₹2,800 cab. Ujjain central hub hai — dono day trips possible hain. 5-day circuit: Ujjain (2 days) → Nalkheda (day trip) → Omkareshwar (day trip). Cab booking: WhatsApp +91 97547 09571.
Kaal Bhairav temple is unique because the deity is offered liquor (madira) as prasad — the idol is believed to actually consume it. This ancient Tantric tradition has continued for centuries. The temple is considered the guardian of Ujjain city. Timings: 6AM–10PM. Located in North Ujjain, ~2 km from Mahakaleshwar.
Ujjain Kumbh Mela (Simhastha) har 12 saal mein aata hai. Agle Kumbh Mela ki prediction: 2028. Isse pehle 2016 mein hua tha. Kumbh Mela Ram Ghat, Shipra River par aayojit hota hai. Lagbhag 7 crore devotees aate hain is mahaparva mein.
Yes! Maa Harsiddhi temple in Ujjain is one of the 51 Shaktipeethas — where Sati's elbow (kohni) fell. It is also significant to King Vikramaditya's legend. The famous Deepmalas (oil lamp towers) are lit on Navratri, creating a spectacular sight. Timings: 6AM–10PM.
