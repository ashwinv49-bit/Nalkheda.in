About Omkareshwar Dham

🏝️
Mandhata Island
The island naturally forms the sacred OM symbol when viewed from above — a divine manifestation. The Narmada and Kaveri rivers embrace the island from all sides.
⚡
Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga
The 4th of 12 Jyotirlingas — considered self-manifested (Swayambhu). Climb 84 steps to reach the sanctum. Darshan: 5AM–12:30PM & 4PM–10PM.
🌊
Holy Narmada River
Narmada is considered even more sacred than Ganga in some Shaiva traditions. A ritual parikrama by boat is performed by all pilgrims.
🔱
Mamleshwar Temple
Located on the south bank opposite the island. The southern manifestation of the Jyotirlinga. Both temples must be visited for complete darshan.

What To Do

6 Sacred Experiences
at Omkareshwar

01
⛵
Narmada Boat Parikrama
Circumnavigate the sacred Mandhata island by boat. The OM shape is visible from the water. Most spiritually significant activity. ₹50–100 per person. Available all day.
02
⚡
Jyotirlinga Darshan
Climb the 84 sacred steps to the main temple. Morning 5AM–7AM is least crowded. Abhishek (ritual bathing) can be booked through temple priests.
03
🌅
Narmada Ghat Aarti
Evening aarti at 8:30PM on the Narmada banks. Temple lights reflected in the river create a magical atmosphere. One of MP's most beautiful aarti experiences.
04
🚶
Island Parikrama on Foot
Walk the 6 km circumambulation path around the island. 2–3 hours, 68 minor temples, sacred spots. Best done early morning barefoot.
05
🔱
Mamleshwar Abhishek
Book a special abhishek at Mamleshwar temple on the south bank. Complete the Jyotirlinga darshan with this essential second temple visit.
06
🌊
Holy Narmada Bath
Bathing at Omkareshwar ghat at dawn is highly auspicious. Narmada Jayanti (Jan–Feb) draws lakhs. Collect sacred Narmada water to take home.

ओंकारेश्वर — नर्मदा का पवित्र द्वीप

ओंकारेश्वर मध्य प्रदेश के खंडवा जिले में नर्मदा नदी पर स्थित एक पवित्र द्वीप है जिसे मांधाता द्वीप कहते हैं। यह द्वीप ऊपर से देखने पर पवित्र ॐ (ओम) के आकार का दिखता है, जिसे एक दैवीय चमत्कार माना जाता है। यहाँ 12 ज्योतिर्लिंगों में से चौथा ज्योतिर्लिंग — ओंकारेश्वर महादेव — विराजमान है।

द्वीप पर मुख्य मंदिर है और दक्षिणी तट पर ममलेश्वर मंदिर है। पूर्ण दर्शन के लिए दोनों मंदिरों में जाना आवश्यक है। नर्मदा नदी में नाव द्वारा द्वीप की परिक्रमा करना यहाँ का सबसे पवित्र अनुभव है। ओंकारेश्वर उज्जैन से मात्र 130 किमी और इंदौर से 77 किमी की दूरी पर है।

Frequently Asked Questions — Omkareshwar

🌊 Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga — Jyotirlinga #4, located on the OM-shaped Mandhata Island in the Narmada river. Darshan: 5AM–12:30PM & 4PM–10PM. Boat parikrama: ₹50–100. Nearest hub: Ujjain (130 km). From Indore airport: 77 km (~1.5 hrs).

Omkareshwar Jyotirlinga darshan timing:
• Morning: 5:00 AM – 12:30 PM
• Evening: 4:00 PM – 10:00 PM
• Morning aarti: 5:30 AM
• Evening aarti: 8:30 PM
Arrive by 5AM for sunrise darshan — least crowded and most peaceful.
Narmada boat parikrama: ₹50–₹100 per person. Available all day. Duration: ~30–45 minutes. The OM shape of Mandhata island is visible from the boat — a divine experience. This is the most important activity at Omkareshwar. Boats are available at the main ghat near the temple.
Indore se Omkareshwar: ~77 km, ~1.5 hours. Cab cost: ₹1,500–₹2,000. Route: NH 3 or NH 52. Direct cab from Indore airport to Omkareshwar available. Ujjain se: ~130 km, ~2.5 hours, ₹2,000–₹2,800. For cab booking: WhatsApp +91 97547 09571.
The sacred island is called Mandhata Island. When viewed from above, it naturally forms the shape of OM (ॐ) — considered a divine manifestation. The Narmada and Kaveri rivers embrace the island from all sides. The island has the Jyotirlinga temple on top and 68 minor temples throughout.
Omkareshwar stay options: Narmada Retreat — river view, AC, ₹1,200–₹2,500/night. Temple guest houses near Mamleshwar — ₹1,000–₹1,500/night. Dharamshalas — ₹300 onwards. Ujjain mein better hotels available at better prices — day trip from Ujjain recommended for budget travelers.
Omkareshwar is unique for 3 reasons: (1) OM-shaped island — visible from aerial view, considered a natural divine miracle. (2) Two Jyotirlinga temples in one place — Omkareshwar + Mamleshwar (both required for complete darshan). (3) Narmada parikrama by boat — circumambulating the sacred island on the holy Narmada river.
Maha Shivaratri at Omkareshwar is one of the most spectacular events in MP. Lakhs of devotees gather for all-night vigil and darshan. The temple is lit up brilliantly. Boat parikrama continues through the night. Book accommodation 3–4 months in advance for Maha Shivaratri. Next date: February 2027.
